Piano Lessons

We offer piano for all ages, beginning at age three. Whether you are a beginner or have been playing piano for a while, we have a program to suit your needs. Learn new techniques, theory, harmony, and tone. Learning how to play piano helps develop hand-eye coordination and self-confidence.

Guitar Lessons

We teach acoustic guitar, electric guitar, bass guitar, and ukulele. Whether you are a beginner or experienced musician, our instructors can help you improve your musical abilities and confidence.

Violin Lessons

We offer violin lessons for all ages, beginning at age six. Our violin lessons include a variety of musical styles, from fiddle to classical. Our instructors will help you fine-tune techniques and accomplish your musical goals. We work with you to create a customized program tailored to your needs.
